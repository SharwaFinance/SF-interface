FAQ

What Is Sharwa.Finance?

Sharwa.Finance is a crypto prime brokerage protocol enabling margin spot and options trading with up to 10x leverage. Sharwa isn't the counterparty for traders; it simply allows traders to borrow up to 10 times more capital than they have and ensures that all orders between spot and options DeFi exchanges are executed while ensuring that all liabilities are met.

What is business model for Sharwa?

The business model for Sharwa centers on acting as a prime brokerage protocol, where it generates revenue by ensuring that all trades are executed properly and liabilities are met. The main sources of revenue for Sharwa include:
  • Spreads on Interest RatesSharwa may charge a differential between the interest rates for borrowers and lenders, profiting from the spread.
  • Clearing FeesSharwa collects fees for opening and closing orders, which contribute to its revenue. These fees are charged to facilitate the trading process and cover the operational costs associated with trade execution and settlement.
  • Referral RewardsBy routing option orders through different option markets, Sharwa.Finance earns referral rewards.

What sets Sharwa.Finance apart?

  • 10x Leverage on UniswapSharwa.Finance enables up to 10x leverage on Uniswap, leveraging deep liquidity available on the Arbitrum network.
  • Cross-Margin with American-style OptionsOffering advanced trading, Sharwa.Finance uses the intrinsic value of American-style options as collateral, reducing liquidation risks even with high leverage.
  • Multi-Collateral SystemAllows traders to utilize their portfolio's USDC value for trading, providing significant leverage without requiring asset liquidation.
  • Deep Liquidity and Fair PricingSharwa.Finance ensures traders access to deep liquidity and fair prices by aggregating liquidity from various markets, facilitating better trading conditions without extra fees.

How can I benefit from Sharwa.Finance, if I’m not a trader?

Even if you're not actively trading, you can still contribute capital to our liquidity pools and earn attractive APY returns.

Does Sharwa.Finance have its own token?

No, Sharwa.Finance does not have a token at this time.
